Privacy Policy — Double Duty - Twins Tracker

Effective date: November 23, 2026
Developer: CheefyDev
Contact: cheefy.dev@gmail.com

The short version

Double Duty collects nothing. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no servers receiving your data. Everything you log stays on your own device.

What the app stores

Feeding, sleep, diaper, health and other care logs you enter, plus your settings, are stored locally on your device only. You can export your data at any time from within the app, and deleting the app deletes its data.

Partner sync

If you pair with a partner's phone, data is exchanged directly between your two devices over Bluetooth. It never passes through us or any third party.

Barcode database

Product lookups use a database bundled inside the app (data from Open Food Facts and Open Products Facts contributors, licensed under the Open Database License). Lookups happen on your device; nothing is sent anywhere.

What we receive

Nothing automatically. If you choose to email us (for example via Report a bug), we receive your email address and whatever you send, used only to respond and provide support.

External links

The app may link to external services (for example, a donation page). Those services have their own privacy policies; we do not control them.

Children

Double Duty is designed for parents and caregivers (adults). Information about children is entered by their parents or caregivers and remains on their devices under their control.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
